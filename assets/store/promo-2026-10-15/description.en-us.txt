Multiverse Doomsday is a co-op tower defense: recruit heroes, unleash leader powers and protect your base from waves of enemies and bosses.

BUILD YOUR SQUAD
Collect 43 heroes and combine damage, control and support. Place your defenses, upgrade them during battle and choose your leader's power.

CHOOSE YOUR CHALLENGE
Defend 6 maps in the campaign, survive endless waves in Competitive or build a new strategy with Card Roguelike. Try Quick mode for a shorter run.

GROW YOUR TEAM
Earn Gold, complete missions, open chests with visible odds and develop heroes, bases and commander talents. Collect daily and seasonal rewards.

Play solo or coordinate a team of up to 4 players. Start with training and take on the next wave together!
