Build your hero squad and defend the multiverse in this cooperative tower defense! Play solo or team up with up to 4 players.

RECRUIT AND EVOLVE
Collect 43 heroes, combine abilities and choose your squad's upgrades. Earn heroes through gameplay, complete quests and open chests with visible odds.

DEFEND EVERY WORLD
Explore 6 maps, face bosses and protect your base. Progress through the campaign, try card mode and compete on the ranked leaderboard.

YOUR NEXT GOAL
Level up your commander, customize your base and earn daily and seasonal rewards.

HOW TO PLAY
Place heroes near the path, upgrade your defense and press Q to activate your leader's power. On mobile, use the on-screen controls.

Invite your friends, plan together and survive the next wave!
